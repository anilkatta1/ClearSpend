# Anonymized Sales pipeline

**Owner:** Niraj Gupta  
**Status:** research participants only; no evidence-backed qualified opportunity currently recorded

## Stage definitions

1. **Research participant:** completed workflow discovery; not automatically a prospect.
2. **Qualified:** target-segment fit, material problem, purchase authority, approximate volume, and next step confirmed.
3. **Evaluation scheduled:** participant agrees to a dated guided product evaluation.
4. **Offer presented:** the same exact pilot package and price are presented to a qualified buyer.
5. **Pilot accepted:** the buyer signs, issues a purchase order, or pays under the defined evidence standard.
6. **Pilot active/completed:** usage, value, safety, support, and renewal evidence are measured.

## Pipeline metrics

- Qualified buyers contacted
- Guided evaluations scheduled and completed
- Exact offers presented
- Accepted and rejected offers
- Primary objection or loss reason
- Discounts or custom work requested
- Days between stages
- Pilot activation and renewal outcome

Advance a stage only when the participant takes the corresponding action. Do not represent research participation or general interest as a qualified opportunity.
