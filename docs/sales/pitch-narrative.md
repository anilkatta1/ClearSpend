# ClearSpend pitch narrative

**Owner:** Niraj Gupta  
**Target:** 10–12 slides plus a live product walkthrough

## 1. Title and promise

ClearSpend provides trustworthy reimbursement review with a human in control.

## 2. Observed customer problem

Finance reviewers reconstruct receipt, policy, approval-context, and decision evidence across separate tools and people. Current evidence is qualitative; do not show an invented time-saving figure.

## 3. Insight

Reviewers need a prepared, inspectable evidence packet. The AI recommendation supports the decision but does not own it.

## 4. Product workflow

Show receipt upload, quarantine and scan, employee verification, deterministic checks, bounded AI assistance, human review, accounting-code confirmation, CSV export, and audit history.

## 5. Live demo

Use only the synthetic hotel, taxi, and train receipts. Demonstrate one same-category report, evidence checks, policy citations, an information request or decision, accounting handoff, and audit verification.

## 6. Trust and failure behavior

Objective rules run deterministically. AI has no approval, payment, policy-changing, or export authority. Missing data and provider failure route to human review. Receipt files are scanned, encrypted, tenant-scoped, and versioned.

## 7. Research evidence and changed decisions

- I-01 supports visible policy context, inline receipt preview, information-request continuity, human authority, and retained history.
- I-02 through I-04 show why ClearSpend must not claim to replace budget, procurement, approval, or payment systems.
- I-05 remains internal context and does not count as independent customer validation.

## 8. Initial segment

Working hypothesis: small and mid-sized Indian organizations reviewing recurring employee reimbursements through fragmented tools. Segment frequency, urgency, and buyer authority remain unvalidated.

## 9. Business model and pricing

Show the guided evaluation followed by the active ₹25,000 INR, paid-in-advance, 30-calendar-day design-partner pilot for one organization, one configured reimbursement-policy workflow, up to 10 active reviewers, unlimited employee submitters, and CSV accounting handoff. Explain that usage is measured for future pricing and unit economics, but the pilot itself is fixed-fee. Label ₹25,000 as an unvalidated discovery offer.

## 10. Alternatives

Compare manual tools, ticketing/project systems, accounting software, broad spend suites, and generic AI. State where each alternative remains stronger.

## 11. Evidence-gated roadmap

Prioritize independent product observations, measured reviewer behavior, buyer responses, and production controls before integrations or broader workflows.

## 12. Ask

Seek qualified finance or operations design partners willing to complete a guided synthetic-data evaluation and, if the product and safety gates pass, consider the ₹25,000 paid-pilot offer.

## Demo closing

> ClearSpend did not approve or pay this reimbursement. It prepared and preserved the evidence. The authorized reviewer made the decision, and the export records an accounting handoff.
