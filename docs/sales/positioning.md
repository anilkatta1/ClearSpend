# ClearSpend positioning

**Owner:** Niraj Gupta  
**Status:** evidence-informed working position; segment and demand remain unvalidated

## Positioning statement

For finance and operations teams at small and mid-sized Indian organizations that review employee reimbursements through fragmented forms, messages, spreadsheets, or ticketing tools, ClearSpend is a human-controlled reimbursement-review assistant that prepares receipt and policy evidence, explains its recommendation, and preserves the final decision. Unlike a generic AI chatbot or a broad spend-management suite, ClearSpend uses deterministic checks first, limits AI to advisory assistance, and ends at a recorded accounting CSV rather than moving money.

## Customer roles

- **Economic buyer:** finance controller, finance-operations lead, CFO, founder, or operations leader with pilot-budget authority.
- **Primary user:** finance reviewer or accountant.
- **Submitting user:** employee requesting reimbursement.
- **Governance user:** policy owner, auditor, or finance leader reconstructing a decision.

These roles are hypotheses until purchase authority and usage are confirmed with target organizations.

## Customer job

Reach a defensible reimbursement decision without repeatedly gathering receipts, policy text, prior approvals, and explanations from separate systems or people.

## Evidence supporting the position

- I-01 describes repeated receipt, policy, and approval-context checking and supports inline receipt preview, policy visibility, human authority, information-request continuity, and an audit history.
- I-02 through I-04 show that authorization, verification, budget context, and reconciliation vary by workflow and organization.
- The implemented product proves the bounded submission, assessment, human-decision, information-request, export, and audit flow with synthetic data.

## Proof available today

- Working local product with receipt security, deterministic checks, bounded AI, human review, information request/resubmission, accounting CSV, and audit history.
- Automated unit, integration, evaluation, and synthetic smoke-test assets.
- One independent finance-reviewer product-feedback record plus adjacent workflow-discovery evidence.

## Claims prohibited by current evidence

- Validated reviewer-time or labor savings.
- Validated willingness to pay or repeatable demand.
- Autonomous approval, payment, or reimbursement completion.
- ERP acceptance or production customer-data readiness.
- Replacement of every approval hierarchy, procurement process, or budget system.

## Short Sales message

> ClearSpend gives a finance reviewer a prepared evidence packet containing receipt checks, the applicable policy, a clearly labeled recommendation, and the history needed to explain the outcome. The authorized reviewer remains responsible for the decision.
