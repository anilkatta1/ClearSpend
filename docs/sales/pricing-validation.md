## Sales offer under test

Sales will use one exact offer in every qualified buyer conversation after the workflow demonstration:

- **Price:** ₹25,000 INR, paid in advance and non-refundable, for a 30-calendar-day design-partner pilot.
- **Organization scope:** one organization and one configured reimbursement-policy workflow.
- **Access:** up to 10 active reviewers and unlimited employee submitters for the pilot workflow.
- **Included workflow:** receipt evidence capture and preview, employee-confirmed claim details, deterministic receipt and policy checks, bounded AI assistance, policy citations, human review and information requests, audit history, CSV accounting handoff, assisted setup, email support, and one end-of-pilot review.
- **Excluded:** custom ERP integrations, payment execution, card issuing, SSO, and production-security commitments beyond the documented MVP scope.
- **Usage:** fixed-fee pilot; usage is measured for learning and future pricing, not for automatic overages.

This is a test offer, not validated market pricing. It supersedes the earlier low-price pilot and provisional monthly-renewal hypotheses.

## Countable willingness-to-pay evidence

A countable result requires:

- Participant role and purchase authority
- Exact package and exact price presented
- Accept, reject, or escalate response
- Main reason or objection
- Discount or customization requested
- Concrete next action and date
- Signed agreement, purchase order, or payment when claiming a paid commitment

Compliments, survey intent, willingness to attend a free evaluation, and non-binding interest do not count as willingness to pay.

## Unit economics to measure

```text
contribution
= collected price
- receipt extraction and OCR cost
- AI invocation rate × AI cost per invocation
- storage, hosting, and export cost
- onboarding and support hours × loaded hourly cost
- payment fees
- expected correction or remediation cost
```

Measure observed usage and support burden during the pilot. Illustrative calculations are not customer or cost evidence.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Unattended free trial:** rejected while the product remains a synthetic-data MVP and policy setup needs assistance.
- **Published recurring SaaS price:** rejected until buyer value, usage, support burden, and preferred metric are measured.
- **Per-seat-only pricing:** risky because occasional approvers, auditors, and submitters should not be discouraged from participation.
- **Pure per-claim pricing:** risky because retries, edits, and information-request cycles should not create surprising charges.
- **Outcome pricing:** rejected until the customer and ClearSpend can independently recompute the business outcome.
