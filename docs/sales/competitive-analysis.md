# Competitive alternatives

**Owner:** Niraj Gupta

| Alternative | Why teams use it | Where it is stronger | ClearSpend advantage | ClearSpend limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email, chat, and spreadsheets | Familiar, flexible, and already available | No migration or procurement effort | Consolidated evidence, repeatable checks, visible rationale, and retained history | Requires workflow and policy setup |
| Ticketing and project tools | Existing approvals, attachments, and project codes | Embedded in established operational processes | Reimbursement-specific receipt and policy evidence | Does not replace budget/project systems or arbitrary approval chains |
| Accounting software | Ledger, reconciliation, and downstream reporting | Authoritative accounting records and controls | Prepares reviewer evidence before accounting handoff | CSV export does not prove ERP acceptance |
| Full spend-management suites | Cards, accounts payable, controls, and integrations | Broader product coverage and mature ecosystems | Narrow, explainable reimbursement workflow with explicit human authority | Much narrower product with fewer integrations |
| Generic AI assistant | Flexible natural-language interaction | Broad summarization and question answering | Deterministic rules, pinned policy citations, human decision, and auditable state | Intentionally constrained and less conversational |

## Competitive position

ClearSpend should compete first against manual evidence reconstruction. Ramp is an incumbent and category reference, not a brand, interface, or feature set to clone. ClearSpend's credible differentiation is its bounded reimbursement workflow, deterministic-first checks, human-owned decisions, and explicit product limits.

## Evidence gap

No interview currently records a buyer choosing ClearSpend over a named alternative, replacing paid software, or quantifying switching cost. This document is positioning logic until direct buyer-comparison evidence is recorded.
