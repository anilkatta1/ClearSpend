# Interview Notes & Proof: Employee Logistics & Relocation Reimbursement
**Scope:** New Joiner Relocation & Logistics Expense Management  
**Key Stakeholders:** New Employee, HR Team, Approved Logistics Vendors, Approving Managers, Finance Team  
**Key Tools & Systems:** OpenAir (Reimbursement Ticketing), HR Approval Records, Empanelled Vendor List, Original Invoices  

---

## Executive Summary

This process outlines how the organization handles relocation and logistics expenses for new employees joining the company. The workflow ensures financial control through pre-agreed budgets, standardized empanelled vendors, digital claim submission via OpenAir, and multi-tier approval chains before final finance settlement.

---

## Detailed Operational Process Breakdown

### 1. Onboarding Approval & Relocation Budget Capping
During the recruitment and onboarding discussion, the HR team and hiring manager define the employee's eligibility for logistics and relocation support.
* A specific relocation budget cap is agreed upon based on company policy and candidate negotiation.
* Formal email confirmation/offer documentation serves as the base authorization for the expense.

### 2. Approved Vendor Allocation & Service Booking
To maintain service reliability and cost standardization, the company provides the incoming employee with an **Empanelled/Approved Vendor List**.
1. The employee reaches out to the approved vendors on the list to discuss availability and service terms.
2. The employee selects the vendor that best meets their timeline and budget parameters.
3. The booking is confirmed, and relocation logistics are executed.

### 3. Service Execution & Receipt Collection
Upon completion of the relocation service:
* The employee pays or receives the final bill from the vendor.
* The employee must obtain **original, itemized invoices** and proof of payment (payment receipts/bank transaction logs).

### 4. Claim Logging in OpenAir
After the logistics movement is complete, the employee initiates the reimbursement request:
* The employee logs into **OpenAir** and creates a dedicated **Reimbursement Ticket**.
* The user uploads scanned copies of all **original bills, vendor invoices, and the initial HR approval document**.
* Expense fields (amount, date, vendor name, category) are filled out and submitted for review.

### 5. Multi-Level Approval Chain & Financial Settlement
Once submitted in OpenAir, the ticket enters a structured approval matrix:
1. **Managerial / HR Review:** Validates that the claim stays within the pre-approved joining budget and that an approved vendor was used.
2. **Operations / Admin Verification:** Cross-checks invoice authenticity and compliance with company travel/relocation policies.
3. **Finance Processing:** Upon receiving all necessary system sign-offs, the Finance team audits the submission and releases the reimbursed amount directly to the employee's designated bank account.

---

## Compliance & Control Highlights

* **Pre-Approved Spending:** No reimbursement is processed without baseline HR/Management eligibility approval logged during onboarding.
* **Empanelled Vendor Usage:** Prevents inflated pricing and ensures service accountability by restricting options to vetted logistics partners.
* **OpenAir Audit Trail:** Centralizes all invoice copies, approval timestamps, and line-item breakdowns in a single auditable system.
