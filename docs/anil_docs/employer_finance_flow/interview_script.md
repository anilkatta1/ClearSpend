# Finance-team interview guide

- **Interview date:** 21 September 2026
- **Interviewer:** Anil Katta
- **Participant category:** finance-team member
- **Method:** informal semi-structured interview
- **Record type:** retrospective reconstruction from the topics Anil reports discussing; not a claim that these questions were read word-for-word

## Purpose

Understand the organization's existing employee-reimbursement flow and obtain finance-side feedback on whether ClearSpend addresses meaningful work in that process.

## Reconstructed discussion guide

### Existing process

1. Can you walk me through what happens after an employee submits a reimbursement and receipts?
2. Which people or roles normally review or approve the reimbursement?
3. What does each person verify before approving it?
4. How are receipts opened, downloaded and compared with the submitted claim?
5. How do reviewers confirm that the expense was within an approved budget?
6. How do reviewers find and apply the relevant reimbursement policy?
7. Which steps are repeated by different people, and where does the process usually take the most time?
8. What evidence is available later if finance or an auditor needs to understand the decision?

### ClearSpend discussion

After discussing or demonstrating ClearSpend, explore:

1. Would automated receipt and policy checks remove meaningful manual work from the current process? Why or why not?
2. Is previewing receipts inside the review screen preferable to downloading them individually?
3. Is displaying the applicable policy and explanation beside the receipt useful for a reviewer?
4. Does retaining receipt history, policy checks and human decisions make the process more useful for audit?
5. Is it important that a human reviewer remains responsible for approval or rejection?
6. If a receipt is missing, is continuing the same case after requesting information preferable to restarting the reimbursement?
7. What would still prevent the finance team from using this workflow in the organization?

Question 7 is retained as the recommended closing question for future interviews. No answer is attributed to the 21 September participant because Anil's supplied notes did not include one.

## Evidence-handling note

Record only the participant's role category and anonymized findings. Do not include names, client information, receipt contents or internal financial data. Before an interview intended for submission evidence, state how the anonymized summary will be used and record the participant's verbal or written consent.
