# Reimbursement-review interview guide

**Method:** informal, semi-structured discussion. The guide is reconstructed from the topics recorded in the interview notes; it is not a verbatim questionnaire.

## Current workflow

1. What happens after an employee submits a reimbursement and receipts?
2. Which roles review it, and what does each role verify?
3. How are receipts, budget approval, and policy rules checked today?
4. Which checks are repeated, delayed, or difficult to audit?
5. What happens when evidence is missing or unclear?

## ClearSpend concept discussion

1. Would a unified receipt, policy, and approval-context view remove meaningful work? Why or why not?
2. Is inline receipt preview preferable to downloading files individually?
3. Are visible policy citations and explanations useful during review?
4. Must a human retain the approval/rejection decision?
5. Would continuing the same case after an information request be preferable to restarting?
6. What would prevent adoption (approval hierarchy, budget-system integration, exceptions, audit retention, security, or price)?

## Evidence handling

State the intended anonymized use before the discussion; record consent; retain only role category and non-identifying findings. Do not store raw receipts, client information, financial figures, recordings, or contact information in the repository.
