# INT-05 — Relocation reimbursement stakeholder

**Evidence type:** Independent process-discovery summary  
**Consent:** Participant consented to anonymized sharing for this assignment.  
**Scope:** Employee logistics and relocation reimbursement.

## What the participant described

The participant described relocation support that begins with an approved cap during onboarding, uses approved vendors, requires itemized invoices and proof of payment, and proceeds through a reimbursement ticket and multi-role review before finance settlement.

## Product implications

- A reimbursement workflow needs evidence capture, a policy/budget reference, receipt-quality checks, and a transparent human-review record.
- Approved-vendor enforcement, onboarding eligibility, and payment remain organization-owned controls outside the current MVP.

## Boundary

This is reimbursement-related workflow discovery, not a ClearSpend usability test, product-preference study, or willingness-to-pay signal.
