# INT-03 — Head of operations

**Evidence type:** Independent process-discovery summary  
**Consent:** Participant consented to anonymized sharing for this assignment.  
**Scope:** Internal operating-expense governance.

## What the participant described

The participant described quarterly budget planning in a spreadsheet, requisitions and invoice attachments in a ticketing system, and project/client cost mapping in a project-cost system. Fixed facilities costs, routine operating expenses, and project-linked spending follow different approval paths. Routine spend is ticket-first; project spend is linked to a project code and requires project and operations sign-off. Weekly reconciliation and monthly variance review compare operational records with the budget baseline.

## Product implications

- Reimbursement evidence should retain budget and policy context.
- Exceptions should route to a human.
- The system should support an auditable reconciliation trail.
- ClearSpend must not claim to replace organizational approval or budget systems.

## Boundary

This is process discovery, not a ClearSpend usability test, product-preference study, or willingness-to-pay signal.
