**Evidence type:** Independent process-discovery summary  
**Consent:** Participant consented to anonymized sharing for this assignment.  
**Scope:** Asset procure-to-pay controls.

## What the participant described

The participant described a controlled asset-purchasing flow: management justification and approval, a purchase request and competing quotations, purchase-order issuance, delivery/specification verification, invoice payment, and debit/credit-note reconciliation for returns or changes.

## Product implications

- Preserve a traceable chain between authorization, evidence, verification, and adjustment.
- Keep the relationship between evidence and financial controls auditable.
- Do not infer that ClearSpend supports purchase orders, procurement, asset management, or payment execution; its current scope is reimbursement review.

## Boundary

This is adjacent-domain process discovery, not a ClearSpend product evaluation or willingness-to-pay signal.
