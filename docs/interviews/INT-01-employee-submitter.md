# INT-01 — Frequent employee reimbursement submitter

**Evidence type:** Independent qualitative product-feedback summary  
**Consent:** Participant consented to anonymized sharing for this assignment.  
**Scope:** Receipt-based employee reimbursement submission and follow-up.

## What the participant described

The participant regularly submits receipt-based reimbursement claims through an existing multi-step workflow. They described repeated receipt downloads and checks, fragmented policy, budget, and approval context, delays while evidence moves between approvers, and difficulty reconstructing a decision later.

## Feedback on ClearSpend

The participant considered a single packet containing receipt previews, confirmed fields, policy results, explanations, and decision history valuable. They valued retaining human review and continuing the same claim when a reviewer requests missing evidence rather than restarting it.

## Product implications

- Keep unified evidence, inline receipt preview, deterministic line matching, and visible policy context.
- Keep human decision ownership and an auditable history.
- Preserve append/resubmit behavior for missing evidence.

## Boundary

This is independent qualitative feedback from one frequent submitting user. It does not measure reviewer time saved, willingness to pay, or production readiness.
