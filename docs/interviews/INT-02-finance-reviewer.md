# INT-02 — Finance-team reviewer

**Evidence type:** Independent qualitative product-feedback summary  
**Consent:** Participant consented to anonymized sharing for this assignment.  
**Scope:** Review of employee reimbursement claims.

## What the participant described

The participant described a reimbursement process in which multiple reviewers repeatedly download receipts, compare merchant, date, and amount details, locate policy text, and contact budget owners or earlier approvers. The rationale can become fragmented across messages and documents.

## Feedback on ClearSpend

The participant considered a unified review packet useful when it presents receipt previews, policy context and explanations, and a recorded recommendation alongside the human decision. They specifically valued avoiding individual receipt downloads, preserving an audit trail, retaining human authority, and allowing missing information to be appended to the same case rather than restarting it.

## Product implications

- Retain inline preview, pinned policy citations, immutable evidence/history, and human decision ownership.
- Preserve the information-request and append/resubmit workflow.

## Boundary

This is direct qualitative feedback from one finance reviewer. It is not a measurement of time saved or a commitment to buy.
