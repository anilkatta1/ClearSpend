**## Commercial decisions and rejected alternatives**



The Sales artifacts record these decisions and their rationale:



**1.** **\*\*Start with reimbursement review.\*\*** Position the product around receipt and policy evidence, human decisions and accounting handoff. A full spend-management or payments proposition would exceed the implemented workflow and available evidence.

**2.** **\*\*Use a defined pilot offer.\*\*** Present one consistent ₹25,000 package. Published recurring SaaS pricing remains deferred until usage, buyer preference and cost-to-serve are better understood; the previous low-price offer is superseded.

**3.** **\*\*Separate feedback from demand.\*\*** Preserve interview findings without treating compliments, free evaluations or unqualified participants as a pipeline or paid traction. Record an exact-price rejection as useful evidence rather than conceal it.

**4.** **\*\*Make delivery effort visible.\*\*** Evaluate onboarding and support costs alongside OCR, AI and hosting. The stress scenario makes the commercial risk inspectable instead of implying an unsupported high-margin business.

**5.** **\*\*Keep financial authority explicit.\*\*** The reviewer decides; AI advises. CSV export does not establish ERP acceptance, payment or completed reimbursement.
